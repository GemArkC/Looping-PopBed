From Co-Sleeper to Infant Crib to Toddler Crib and Playard

Meet the Looping PopBed — a versatile sleep and play space designed to grow with your little one. From newborn days to toddlerhood, PopBed transitions from a co-sleeper to an infant crib, toddler crib, and full-size playpen, giving parents a practical space for rest, play, and everyday moments. Lightweight, washable, and travel-friendly, PopBed is designed to make life with your little one more convenient — at home or on the go. 

THE LOOPING POPBED AT A GLANCE

Versatile From Birth to Toddlerhood
Designed to support your little one's changing needs — from newborn sleeping to toddler rest and playtime.

Co-Sleeping Made Easier
The dropping side panel allows parents to use PopBed as a co-sleeper while keeping baby close.

Zip-On Newborn Bassinet
A dedicated zip-on newborn bassinet provides a cozy space for your little one during the early months.

Extra Comfort
The padded bottom provides added comfort for baby's rest and playtime.

Easy Access
The zipper door access makes it easy for your little one to enter and exit during playtime.

Convenient Diaper Changes
The clip-on diaper changer provides a dedicated space for quick diaper changes.

One-Hand Folding System
The one-hand folding system makes setup and folding more convenient for parents. 

PopBed folds in seconds and becomes compact for easy storage or travel. It comes with a convenient carrying bag to take comfort on the go.

Mesh windows on both sides allow for excellent airglow, keeping your baby cool and comfortable. Perfect for naptime or play, even on warmer days.

Stay close to your little one with dropping front side, designed for easy access at any moment. Especially helpful during nighttime care.

The upper level is suitable from birth up to 6 months (up to 9 kg), keeping your baby closer and easier to reach. For more active toddlers, the lower level provides a safe and comfortable space up to 36 months (up to 15 kg).

Built with sturdy legs that ensure stability and safety during every use. A reliable base for sleep and play, wherever you are.

The removable diaper changer can be attached and detached in seconds, offering added convenience for busy parents. It provides a comfortable and clean changing experience for your little one.
