From Co-Sleeper to Infant Crib to Toddler Crib and Playard

Meet the Looping Popbed — a versatile sleep and play space designed to make everyday parenting more convenient, with an easy open-and-close system that helps simplify setup and storage. From newborn days to toddlerhood, Popbed transitions from a co-sleeper to an infant crib, toddler crib, and full-size playpen, giving parents a practical space for rest, play, and everyday moments. Designed with a focus on convenient setup, Popbed makes it easier to prepare your little one's space at home or pack it away when it's time to go.

THE LOOPING POPBED AT A GLANCE

  • Easy Open and Close
    • Setting up baby gear doesn't have to feel complicated. Popbed features a folding system designed to make opening and closing more convenient for parents.

  • Versatile From Birth to Toddlerhood
    • Designed to adapt to your little one's changing needs, from newborn sleeping to toddler rest and playtime.

  • Co-Sleeping Made Easier
    • The dropping side panel allows Popbed to be configured as a co-sleeper when used according to the manufacturer's instructions.

  • Zip-On Newborn Bassinet
    • A dedicated zip-on newborn bassinet provides a separate space for your little one during the early months.

  • Extra Comfort
    • The padded bottom provides added comfort for baby's rest and playtime.

  • Easy Access
    • The zipper door provides convenient access to the playard when configured for appropriate use.

  • Convenient Diaper Changes
    • The clip-on diaper changer provides a dedicated changing surface for everyday diaper changes.

  • Travel-Friendly Design
    • At 11.8 kg, Popbed is designed to be movable, and the included travel bag makes transporting and storing it more convenient.

PopBed folds in seconds and becomes compact for easy storage or travel. It comes with a convenient carrying bag to take comfort on the go.

Mesh windows on both sides allow for excellent airflow, keeping your baby cool and comfortable. Perfect for naptime or play, even on warmer days.

Stay close to your little one with dropping front side, designed for easy access at any moment. Especially helpful during nighttime care.

The upper level is suitable from birth up to 6 months (up to 9 kg), keeping your baby closer and easier to reach. For more active toddlers, the lower level provides a safe and comfortable space up to 36 months (up to 15 kg).

Built with sturdy legs that ensure stability and safety during every use. A reliable base for sleep and play, wherever you are.

The removable diaper changer can be attached and detached in seconds, offering added convenience for busy parents. It provides a comfortable and clean changing experience for your little one.
