One Popbed, four ways to use it. Made for every stage.

Meet the Looping Popbed, a smart, versatile 4-in-1 sleep and play solution that grows with your little one. From newborn snuggles to toddler adventures, Popbed transforms from a co-sleeper to an infant crib, toddler crib, and full-size playard, adapting to your family's changing needs.

Its easy open-and-close system takes the hassle out of setup and folding. Less time setting up. More time for what matters.

Thoughtful details, everywhere

Compact fold, ready to go

PopBed folds in seconds and becomes compact for easy storage or travel. It comes with a convenient carrying bag.

Breathable mesh panels

Mesh on all sides promotes excellent airflow, creating a well-ventilated space for resting or playing, even on warmer days.

Easy-access drop-down side

Keep your little one close and within easy reach, making nighttime care easier.

Comfort that grows with them

The upper level suits birth to 6 months (up to 9 kg). For active toddlers, the lower level is a safe, comfortable space up to 36 months (up to 15 kg).

Stable support for every moment

Sturdy legs ensure stability and safety during every use. A reliable base for sleep and play, wherever you are.

Quick changes, made easy

The removable diaper changer attaches and detaches in seconds, giving a comfortable, clean changing experience.
